## What changed in the application

- `src/geom/tileTool.ts`: `Mitre` carries the owning face's normal. `mitreReach(mitre, zMin, zMax)` solves the mitre plane against the tool's signed offset interval and returns the physical distance (mm, in the face plane) the tool must continue past the fold: `depth·tan(θ/2)` outside a convex bend, the same for a cut or recess inside a concave valley, nothing on the other side. `toolOffsetRange(mode, depth, wall)` is the single definition of the tool interval (cut `[-(wall+1), 1]`, recess `[-depth, 1]`, emboss `[-0.2, depth]`) used by the layout, the worker, the tests and the harnesses. `mitreTool` takes the offset range and sizes its trim zone from the reach; the zone now ends with the fold instead of running past its ends.
- `src/geom/parameterization.ts`: `directionalScale(t, dx, dy)` gives mm per uv unit in a chosen direction from the triangle's affine map. Exact on planar faces, and the right metric for a distance perpendicular to an edge on a stretched flattening.
- `src/geom/layout.ts`: `LayoutSettings.foldExtend` (a scalar mistaken for uv units) is replaced by `normalRange`. Each fold edge gets its own physical reach, converted to uv through the directional metric perpendicular to the edge; the copy padding follows the largest reach. Margin bands are `margin` millimetres wide on the surface at every scale, and at a fold corner a real edge's band continues past the corner, so the strip that carries the pattern past a fold cannot bypass the rim margin. Reach above `MAX_FOLD_REACH` (20 mm, a bend near 180°) throws `Fold too sharp: …` instead of clamping. A margin that covers a piece entirely (a 1.6 mm rim with a 2.5 mm margin) is logged. Ring closure: `toolMitres(flat, layout)`, `LayoutResult.closureJoined`, `stretchY`, and a seam fit along the tile's height for 90 / 270° wraps.
- `src/geom/regionFlatten.ts`: after the unfolding tree is built, `closeRings` looks at the folds left over between pieces of one sheet. A fold whose two sides coincide after a plain translation, whose loop through the tree has every fold parallel to it and passes through no ring-shaped (seam-cut) piece, becomes the sheet's closure: every piece of the sheet gets the translation as its `period`, and the two end pieces get `closure` (edges, vertices, mitre). Turns (a cube corner's third edge: 90°), loops through a rim, second rings on one sheet, and non-straight folds are logged as cut with the reason. The layout joins the closure only when whole repeats fit around the period at the chosen rotation.
- `src/worker/geom.worker.ts`, `protocol.ts`, `TilePanel.tsx`: the worker reports `parts` (positive-volume components after island removal) and logs the removed island volume; the panel raises an error notification when more than one part remains and surfaces the closure notes; the tool is mitred at a joined closure.

## Evidence and attribution

### Dimensional probes (`expanded/after-final/probes.json`, before: `ridge-probe.json`, `diagnostics.jsonl`)

Filled-tile controls, 0.8 mm emboss: ridge apex error at 45 / 90 / 135 / -90° and scales 0.6 / 1 / 1.7 is below 1e-7 mm in all 12 cases (before: up to 1.57 mm). Box corner at scales 0.6 / 1 / 1.7: emboss `(50.8, -0.8)`, recess `(49.2, 0.8)`, cut removes the corner; error ≤ 1e-6 mm. Physical margin: pattern spans z 2.5 to 47.5 at every scale and mode; on the final solid the front wall is at its original level at z 47.6 and z 2.4 and modified at z 47.4 and z 2.6.

Images: `expanded/after-final/images/box-corner-scale0.6-emboss.png` and `plate-135-emboss.png` (before left, after right, same camera), `ring-<pattern>-<mode>.png` (whole ring and the magnified closing edge).

### The 41 angled-plate flags (`expanded/after-final/angles-*.jsonl`, 522 runs)

The harness runs every pattern on 1.6 mm plates at 45°, 135° and a -90° valley in all modes, on two fixtures: the audit's original plates (`gap`) and corrected plates clipped at the bisector (`mitred`). Each run also measures a filled-tile control on the same solid and repeats the crease comparison 1 mm from the fold.

- The original -90° fixture was two plates touching only along their top line, with a V-shaped gap underneath (verified by slicing: the plates' inner edges run from the ridge down to `(±0.612, -1.478)`). The 12 cut flags and 2 recess flags on that plate come from the two separate plates plus island removal; on the mitred fixture they are 0 and only the 7 known multi-part cut patterns have more than one part. The same gap existed in `tests/crease.test.ts` and the review harnesses; all are fixed (plates now start 5 mm past the bisector and are clipped there).
- The 26 emboss flags on the -90° valley persist on both fixtures (23 to 27 patterns) while the filled control on the same solid hits the analytic valley apex to 1e-4 mm, and the 1 mm-offset measurement shows no flag at all. Within `depth·tan(45°) = 0.8 mm` of a valley, a ray normal to one face starts inside the neighbouring face's relief; the metric, not the geometry, is at fault there.
- The one 135° flag (unicursalMaze emboss, 1.9 mm) does not recur: it was the sharp-bend underfill.

Every control on both fixtures is within 0.02 mm of its expected apex (0 exceptions in 522 runs).

### Broader geometry (`expanded/before` and `expanded/after-final`, 609 runs each)

All 29 patterns on a cylinder, a rounded box, a three-face corner, the interior, the whole shell, opposite faces and a nonuniform (0.8 mm) shell, in cut / emboss / recess at rotation 37° and scale 0.6: 0 errors and 0 non-`NoError` results before and after. Multi-part results occur only for the seven disconnected cut patterns (plus recess through the 0.8 mm walls of the nonuniform fixture, which is a through-recess by construction). No unexplained 2D crease run on the two-face, cylinder, rounded-box, opposite-face or nonuniform bodies.

The whole-shell, three-face and interior bodies now show 2D crease runs (up to 19.5 mm on the whole shell, 1.75 / 2.5 mm on the corners) that were 0 before. They are the corrected margin: the 1.6 mm rim is blanked entirely by the 2.5 mm band along its unjoined edges while the wall next to it is patterned up to the fold, and a cut third edge of a corner has its band while the joined edges do not. The old 1.5 mm band left a 0.1 mm patterned sliver on the rim that the metric read as continuous. The layout now logs when a margin covers a piece entirely.

### Parameter extremes (`expanded/before/extremes-*.jsonl`, 707 configurations)

Every generator parameter at its minimum and maximum, every style choice, four seeds, five tile sizes at two line widths, and the combined extremes: 0 crashes, 0 seamless-edge failures (edge mismatch ≤ 0.02 mm wherever seamless is expected). 88 configurations produce an empty pattern with a generator warning ("Feature covers under 2% of the tile", "nothing would remain", a rib width below the 0.84 mm minimum feature, tiny tiles) rather than an error; the first run of the harness had mis-recorded these as `TypeError`s because it built a CrossSection from no polygons. Generation code was not changed.

### Ring closure (`expanded/after-final/ring-*.jsonl`, 348 runs)

All 29 patterns × cut / emboss / recess × rotations 0, 90, 180 and 37° on the four outer walls with a 2.5 mm margin: 0 errors, every solid and its STL round trip `NoError`. At 0 / 90 / 180° the closing edge is joined in all 87 runs per rotation; at 37° in none, with the note "the ring's closing edge is left as a cut …". Longest unexplained disagreement across the closing edge: 0 mm in 2D and 0 mm in 3D at every rotation, the same as across the three tree folds (an earlier pass read 0.2 / 1.0 mm until the review helper's outline filter was taught that a joined closure is a fold). Multi-part results: only the disconnected cut patterns (six at 0°, seven at the other rotations).

### Connected cuts (`expanded/after-final/connectivity-*.jsonl`, 168 runs)

For delaunayTile, penroseApproximant, moire, hyperbolic, penrose, truchet and guilloche on the two box walls, cut mode, the picker default and each of invert / Connect material / both toggled, at island cutoffs 0 / 5 / 20 mm³ and scales 1 and 0.6:

- Connect material alone gives **one part in all 42 runs** (both scales, all cutoffs), with no island removed.
- Inversion alone gives one part for delaunayTile, penrose, penroseApproximant and moire (moire's retained ribs fall below 1.2 mm), for hyperbolic only at scale 1 (4 parts at 0.6), and not for guilloche (8 parts at 0.6) or truchet (5 to 13).
- Raising the cutoff to 20 mm³ merely deletes more of the design (up to 2.5 cm³ removed) and still leaves several parts for most of them.

No pattern default was changed. The recommended connected-cut preset for these seven is Connect material on; the app now tells the user when a result is more than one part.

## Supported, unsupported, unresolved

Supported now: physical relief, recess and through-cut to the mitre at any tile scale and rotation; convex bends to about 160° and concave valleys; margins in millimetres on every real edge and at fold ends; four-wall (and rectangular) rings closed at 0 / 90 / 180 / 270° with any origin or root wall; loose-part reporting.

Unsupported by design, reported to the user: ring closure at other rotations or with seam fitting off (left cut, note shown); three faces at a cube vertex (the third edge stays cut, reason logged); a loop through a ring-shaped piece such as the box rim; a second ring on the same sheet (the box's inner walls when the outer ring is chosen); bends whose reach exceeds 20 mm (error).

Unresolved or not done: no connected-cut preset in the UI (evidence says Connect material; a default change needs its own review); the seven patterns still fragment at their defaults; no physical prints or slicer inspection (no slicer here); no browser screenshots; performance and memory budgets not measured beyond the recorded timings; SVG imports with holes and the surface Voronoi workflow not exercised.
